# Prioritized staff usability audit — 13 October 2026

This audit records observed source behavior and the acceptance criteria for a staged tablet walkthrough. The ordering fixes ship in this release. Broader admin navigation and workflow restructuring remain a subsequent release.

| Priority | Observed friction | Affected task | Proposed change / release status | Acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Half selections consumed full units; chicken used the ordinary topping weight; allowances were hard to read. | Price a split pizza or add a named extra topping. | Implemented: shared weighted units, duplicate normalization, recipe reservation and selected/included/extra-charge meter; chicken label. | Customer build, staff build, quote, committed line and receipt agree to the cent across halves, chicken, shared deals and omissions. |
| P0 | Cart lines could be removed or quantity-adjusted but never reopened. | Correct a mistaken topping, drink or burger note. | Implemented: edit/save/cancel for both shared customizers; simple-product note/quantity editor. | Saving replaces one line; cancelling preserves it; all prior choices survive. |
| P0 | A failed quote could leave an older total displayed and placement could run without a successful quote. | Take payment after a rapid edit or a network failure. | Implemented: request-key matching, immediate stale invalidation, quoted line prices, timeout/error/retry states and placement guard. | No stale quote enables payment. Failure and recovery keep the draft. |
| P0 | Section navigation unmounted the till; an accidental refresh/discard lost work. | Look up another admin screen during a phone order. | Implemented: mounted in-memory draft, leave/refresh/discard/logout guards, successful-placement reset. | Return from another section with all order/customer fields intact; cancellation keeps draft. |
| P0 | Cash calculator followed payment and print controls; malformed input was stripped into a different amount. | Collect cash, make change and finish printing. | Implemented: amount due/tender/change before the action, strict cents parser, retained committed amount and explicit last-completed label. | $20 / $16.55 yields $3.45, including post-placement display; gift cards and nickel rounding work. |
| P1 | Placement controls were small; condensed labels were hard to read at arm's length. | Select Whole/Left/Right accurately on a tablet. | Implemented in ordering: 44px controls, larger text, explicit pressed states, focus outlines and veggie/meat/fish grouping. | Confirm dimensions and legibility on the actual tablet; anchovies appear under Meat & fish without changing menu order within a group. |
| P1 | Repeated Pop 1…Pop 4 required separate choice lists. | Take repeated/mixed canned drinks. | Implemented: compatible slot quantity picker and compact summaries. | Four identical cans need repeated + taps in one list; allowance cannot be exceeded; complete slot data reaches the kitchen. |
| P1 | Short viewport/open keyboard can bury payment or the customizer footer. | Add preparation notes and finish the ticket on a phone/tablet. | Implemented: dynamic viewport modal and scrollable body; staged usability verification required. | In portrait/landscape with keyboard open, notes and payment/save actions remain reachable without page overflow. |
| P2 | “History & offers” combines order research with promotion management; the sidebar mixes counter, kitchen, owner and marketing work. | Find past orders or run a promotion while serving. | Next release: responsibility-based navigation; separate order history from promotions. | Counter staff can reach history and order entry in one navigation step; marketing actions have a distinct home and permission checks. |
| P2 | Menu availability depends on active, sold out, setup required, staff-only, fulfilment, category and offer timing in separate places. | Explain why a delivery pizza is absent. | This release repairs the five delivery records through a targeted preview; next release adds a unified availability indicator. | Every hidden item names its blocking reason and channel; owner edits and historical orders remain intact. |
| P2 | Saving/error feedback differs across website, menu, settings, history and integrations. | Know whether a price/configuration change was saved. | Next release: consistent pending/saved/error state and recoverable field-level feedback. | Each save is visibly pending, then saved or failed; an error preserves entered fields and offers retry. |

Staging evidence must identify device/viewport, tested build and database snapshot, each completed task, observed errors, and follow-up owner. Test with counter staff doing a phone delivery, a four-pop deal and a corrected cash order under a queue. Hardware printing and drawer validation require the restaurant's actual devices before release. See the release runbook for the full scenario matrix.

Local Chrome walkthroughs at 768×1024 and 393×852 confirmed saved burger edits, cancellation, section retention, four identical drinks, blocked/recoverable failed quotes, refresh warning and retained committed cash totals. They also revealed unreadable till header controls and an uncaught offline dashboard-refresh error; both were fixed. Actual staff/keyboard/hardware observations remain pending on staging. The [release runbook](STAFF_ORDERING_RELEASE_2026-10-13.md#local-validation-evidence) records the completed checks and remaining acceptance work.
